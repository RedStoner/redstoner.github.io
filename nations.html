Nations Plugin

QuestLines Nations

A city-and-nation governance layer built on top of QuestLines Claims. Players band together into invite-only cities; cities federate into nations. Each tier has its own claim pool, treasury, ranks, and diplomacy.

Elevator Pitch

Two or more players form an invite-only city: name, tag, color, motd. Each player keeps their personal QuestLines Claims chunks unchanged — those stay private — but the city itself owns a separate pool of city chunks for shared builds: markets, mines, halls, anything the membership decides together. Members with the right rank claim and unclaim those chunks through Claims’ map.

Two or more cities federate into an invite-only nation. Cities keep their own land. The nation receives a third pool of nation chunks for federation projects — embassies, shared infrastructure, whatever the member cities cooperate on. The capital city’s mayor is the sole gate for nation-membership changes.

Diplomacy operates at both levels. Cities ally and war other cities; nations ally and war other nations. Member cities of the same nation are implicitly at peace and share visibility. Each tier’s “Allies” rank decides what allied outsiders may do inside its chunks — nations cannot grant outsiders access to their member cities’ chunks, only to the nation’s own land.

The Two Tiers

Tier Members Leader Owns
City Individual players (min 2) Mayor One contiguous group of CITY-typed claims
Nation Member cities (min 2) Capital city’s mayor A pool of NATION-typed claims (multiple groups allowed)

Membership is exclusive at both tiers: a player belongs to at most one city, and a city belongs to at most one nation. Joining a city does not automatically expose your personal claims to your fellow members — that’s a separate opt-in toggle (see Share with City).

Three Kinds of Claim

Every chunk on the map falls into one of four buckets. The first three live in QuestLines Claims as different claim owner types; the fourth is unclaimed wilderness.

Owner type Who claims it Who can build inside Banner color
PLAYER Any player via /claim map The owner; optionally fellow city members if Share with City is on for that claim Player’s city color (if any)
CITY A city member with the CITY_CLAIM rank perm Members of the owning city (per rank); city allies (per Allies rank) City color
NATION A member-city mayor with the NATION_CLAIM rank perm Players in any member city (per nation rank); nation allies (per Allies rank) Nation color
Wilderness — Everyone (subject to Claims’ wilderness flags) None

All three claim flavors share a single map view: open /claim map and switch the owner selector in the top row between Personal, your city, and your nation. Nations does not ship its own map UI — it registers a provider with Claims so the shared map shows the right tiers.

Dependencies

Plugin Role Required?
QuestLines Claims Holds the actual per-chunk claim rows. Nations registers City and Nation as additional ClaimOwnerTypes and gates access through ownership resolvers. Required. Nations refuses to come up without it.
VaultUnlocked Backs founding costs, treasury deposits/withdrawals, and the tax sweep. Optional. Without it, all coin-charging features become no-ops unless require_economy is enabled in config.
QuestLines Core Not used. Nations is independent of the Core quest system. —

Command Roots

Nations ships four top-level command trees. See Commands for the full reference.

Command Who runs it Purpose
/city Players Found, join, manage, and leave cities. Bare /city opens the City Overview UI for your current city.
/nation City mayors Found, join, manage, and leave nations. Capital-mayor gated for invites and kicks.
/citiesadmin Server admins (questlinesnations.admin) Override mayor, set perms, force-disband, unclaim, conquer one city into another.
/nationsadmin Server admins Override capital, end wars, force-disband, conquer one nation into another.

Where Data Lives

All persistent state sits under mods/QuestLinesNations/ in a SQLite database (nations.db) plus a single questlinesnations.conf. Future MySQL/MariaDB support is stubbed in config but not implemented yet.

The database is split into roughly the following tables (full layout in Config & Admin):

  • nations_city + nations_city_member + nations_city_rank: city tier.
  • nations_nation + nations_member_city + nations_nation_rank: nation tier.
  • nations_pending_city_creation / nations_pending_nation_creation: founder-charged invites waiting for the second party to accept.
  • nations_city_alliance / nations_city_war + nation equivalents.
  • nations_player_share_grant: per-chunk “share with my city” toggles.
  • nations_plot_listing: city-plot listings sold or given to members.
  • nations_bank_log: append-only audit of every coin movement.

Chunk-by-chunk claim rows live in QuestLines Claims, not here — Nations writes through to Claims via its API.

Boot Sequence

Nations comes up in phases, logged at INFO at each step. If QuestLines Claims is missing at start time, the protection resolvers and share-grant features stay off and the plugin logs a SEVERE warning.

  1. Phase 1. Open the database, load registries, parse config, wire economy.
  2. Phase 2. Register City + Nation ownership resolvers and the share-grant interceptor with Claims; subscribe to chunk-removal events so share grants get cleaned up automatically. Register the claim-owner and share-option providers so Claims’ map UI knows about the new tiers.
  3. Phase 3. Register the four command trees.
  4. Phase 5. Schedule the city tax, nation tax, and grace-sweep tasks on a single-threaded executor ticking at tax_scan_interval_minutes.
  5. Phase 6. Start the chunk-transition entry banner poll and register the chat-tag listener.

What Nations Does Not Do

  • PvP rules. Wars track status only. Declaring war does not turn anyone’s PvP on, off, or differently — vanilla server rules apply throughout.
  • Block protection on player claims. Personal claims stay under QuestLines Claims’ existing per-player permission model. Joining a city doesn’t grant your fellow members access to your private chunks; that’s an explicit per-claim opt-in.
  • Raiding mechanics. Conquest is admin-staged after the fact, not a live mechanic. There is no built-in siege, capture point, or block-of-loot system.
  • Quest plumbing. Nations has no requirements, actions, or trackers for the Core quest system. The two plugins do not interact.
