Nations Plugin

Claims & Plots

How city and nation claims sit inside QuestLines Claims, how members actually claim chunks, the Share-with-City toggle, and the city-plot listing layer.

Where the Data Lives

Nations does not store chunks. Every claimed chunk in a city or nation pool is a row in QuestLines Claims’ database, tagged with ClaimOwnerType.CITY or ClaimOwnerType.NATION and the owner’s UUID. Nations registers two ownership resolvers with Claims so those rows protect the right people:

  • CityOwnershipResolver — consulted whenever a player tries to act on a chunk owned by some city. It looks up the actor’s city, walks their rank’s CITY_* perms (or the Allies rank if they’re an ally), and returns ALLOW/DENY.
  • NationOwnershipResolver — identical at the nation tier. Acts via the player’s member-city’s nation rank.

Server admins automatically bypass both resolvers as step zero. The resolvers never alter the protection chain for personal claims — the Share-with-City interceptor (below) handles those.

The Shared Map

Claiming and unclaiming for both tiers runs through QuestLines Claims’ own map UI: 

/claim map

Nations registers a ClaimOwnerProvider with Claims so the map’s top owner-selector row shows three modes — Personal (your own claims), your city, and your nation. The map handles all the wand-free, click-a-cell claim/unclaim flow; Nations only contributes which entities you’re allowed to act as.

A few things to note:

  • You can only switch into city mode if you’re in a city; same for nation mode.
  • Claiming for the city requires your rank to have CITY_CLAIM (or ADMIN_OVERRIDE); unclaim requires CITY_UNCLAIM.
  • The nation tier uses NATION_CLAIM / NATION_UNCLAIM on your member-city’s nation rank.
  • The City Overview UI and Nation Overview UI each have an Open Map button that opens /claim map pre-focused on an existing city/nation claim so the selector starts on the right tier.

The old standalone CityClaimMapUI and NationClaimMapUI surfaces were retired in the Phase 2 addon refactor — everything lives in Claims’ map now.

Claim Pools

Each tier has a capacity that scales with member count. The capacity acts as a hard ceiling: the system never auto-unclaims when a city or nation drops members, but it refuses new claims past the cap.

TierFormulaFloor keyPer-unit key
City max(city_claim_min_pool, members * city_claims_per_member) city_claim_min_pool (default 8) city_claims_per_member (default 5)
Nation max(nation_claim_min_pool, memberCities * nation_claims_per_city) nation_claim_min_pool (default 12) nation_claims_per_city (default 8)

Claim attempts are rejected when:

  • The target chunk already belongs to any player, city, or nation.
  • The target world is in excluded_worlds.
  • The entity is in GRACE.
  • The pool is at capacity.

Cities are forced single-territory

The CityOwnershipResolver reports allowsMultipleClaimGroups: false to QL Claims, so a city can only ever have one connected blob of land. Every new city claim has to extend the existing claim group; you can’t have a city “outpost” off in another biome. (Nations report true and can have multiple disconnected groups.)

The require_adjacent_city_claims and require_adjacent_nation_claims config flags are present but currently dormant — the single-territory rule above is what actually constrains city claims.

Share with City

Joining a city does not let other members build on your personal claims. By default your private chunks stay strictly private. The Share with City toggle is the only way to opt in, and it’s per claim — not per chunk and not per city membership.

The toggle lives on the per-claim Settings panel inside the Claims map UI. Nations registers a PersonalClaimShareOptionProvider with Claims so that panel gains a “Share with my city” row whenever the viewer is in a city. Flipping it on inserts a PlayerCityShareGrant row for every chunk in that claim; flipping it off removes them.

The standalone /city share command was removed in the same Phase 2 refactor — the per-claim Settings toggle is the only surface now, since the rule is all-or-nothing across a claim.

What sharing actually grants

With a grant active for a chunk, Nations registers a PlayerClaimAccessInterceptor that, when a fellow city member tries to act on that chunk, gates their action by their own rank’s CITY_* perms instead of by the chunk owner’s personal Claims perms. Concretely:

  • If their rank has CITY_BUILD, they can place blocks in your shared chunk.
  • If their rank has CITY_BREAK, they can break blocks.
  • If their rank has CITY_INTERACT, they can right-click stuff.
  • If their rank has CITY_USE_CONTAINERS, they can open your chests.

Outsiders, allies, and other non-members go through the normal Claims personal-claim chain — sharing with the city does not loosen access for anyone else.

Cleanup

Share grants get cleaned up automatically in three cases:

  • You unclaim the underlying personal chunk — Nations subscribes to Claims’ ClaimEventListener.onClaimChunkRemoved and drops the grant.
  • You /city leave — all your grants for that city are dropped.
  • The city disbands — all its grants are dropped.

Grants are stored in nations_player_share_grant keyed by (worldId, chunkX, chunkZ), with the original cityId and playerUuid tracked for revalidation at lookup time.

City Plots

City plots are the “here’s a sub-region of our city for a member to call home” layer. The physical plot definition lives in QuestLines Claims (you wand-select corners and run /claim plot define); Nations layers a listing on top so the mayor can sell or give a plot to a member.

Master switch is plots_enabled (default true) in config — turn it off to disable the entire feature server-wide.

Setup

  1. A holder of MANAGE_PLOTS wand-selects corners inside a city claim and runs /claim plot define <name>. (MANAGE_PLOTS maps to Claims’ MANAGE_SUBREGIONS so the Claims plot subsystem accepts the action.)
  2. Run /city plot list <name> <price> to put the plot up for sale at a fixed price, or /city plot give <name> <player> to assign it free of charge.

Member-facing actions

Any city member can:

  • /city plot browse — open the for-sale browser UI; click Buy to pay price from your Vault balance and have the plot assigned to you.
  • /city plot mine — open your “my plots” page.
  • /city plot info — show the plot under your feet.

Mayor-facing actions

Holders of MANAGE_PLOTS additionally have:

  • /city plot list <name> <price> — price clamped to [min_plot_price, max_plot_price].
  • /city plot unlist <name> — remove a for-sale listing.
  • /city plot give <name> <player> — assign without payment.
  • /city plot revoke <name> — reclaim the plot. If it was previously sold, it auto-relists at the original price.

max_plots_per_member caps how many plots a single player can own simultaneously across all cities (0 = unlimited; default 0).

Plot assignment is enforced through Claims’ existing ClaimPlot model — the plot owner can build, break, and open containers inside the plot regardless of their rank. Other city members fall back to their normal rank perms for the surrounding city claim.

Map Display

/claim map renders every chunk owner type using the same Claims-side widgets. Nations contributes:

  • Owner display name — resolvers report the city or nation name when you hover a chunk.
  • Owner selector entries — your current city and nation appear in the map’s top selector row, alongside Personal.
  • Share-with-city option — on PLAYER claims you own, the Settings panel grows a “Share with my city” toggle.

The exact tints, border decorations, and enemy-relationship overlays are all decided by Claims itself based on the resolver data it gets back. Wilderness stays untinted.
