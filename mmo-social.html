MMO Plugin

Parties & Friends

Short-lived parties for running content together, and a persistent bidirectional friends list with online status.

Parties

Parties are short-lived groups used for dungeons, LFG, and ad-hoc content. A party is created automatically the first time a player sends an invite, and is destroyed as soon as it would have zero members. Parties have a single leader and a maximum size set in config.party.maxSize (default 5).

Running /party with no arguments opens the party panel UI; /party help prints the full subcommand listing in chat.

Invites use /party invite <player> and the recipient accepts with /party accept or rejects with /party decline. Invites expire after config.party.inviteExpirySeconds seconds (default 60).

Only the leader can /party kick members, /party transfer leadership, or /party disband. A leader who runs /party leave must transfer leadership first (or use disband).

ℹ
Parties are not persisted

Party state is held in memory only. A server restart dissolves every active party. Party-aware features (LFG, dungeon entry) re-form their group on demand from the still-online members.

Friends

Each player has a friend list of other player UUIDs. Friendships are bidirectional: /friend add <player> adds an entry on both sides. /friend remove <player> removes it from both. /friend list prints your friends with online or offline status. The list persists in mods/QuestLinesMMO/friends/<playerUuid>.json.
