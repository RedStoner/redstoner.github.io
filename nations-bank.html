Nations Plugin

Bank & Tax

The treasury at each tier, who can move money in and out, how the tax sweeper works, the audit log behind every transfer, and what happens when VaultUnlocked isn’t installed.

Economy Bridge

Nations integrates with VaultUnlocked through a small bridge that abstracts deposit / withdraw / balance. When VaultUnlocked is present and available, real coins move; when it isn’t, the no-op service stands in. Two paths handle this:

  • Default: require_economy=false (default). Without an economy, the founding-cost charges silently succeed as a free op, treasury deposits and withdrawals refuse to run, and the tax sweeper logs a startup warning and never executes.
  • Strict: require_economy=true. Without an economy, founding a city or nation fails up front with a clear error. Use this on servers where economy is core to your design and you don’t want bypasses if VaultUnlocked goes down.

City Treasury

Every city has a single treasury balance stored as a 64-bit integer. Members with the BANK rank perm deposit and withdraw via: 

/city bank balance              show current treasury (any member)
/city bank deposit <amount>     drains personal funds to the treasury
/city bank withdraw <amount>    drains the treasury to personal funds
/city bank log [page]           paginated audit log (any member)

Reads (balance, log) are open to every city member. Writes (deposit, withdraw) require BANK. Withdraw refuses if the treasury can’t cover the amount, and rolls back if the corresponding Vault deposit to the player fails.

Nation Treasury

The nation treasury works the same way, scoped to the nation tier. Any member-city mayor can balance and log; the nation BANK permission is required for movements. 

/nation bank balance
/nation bank deposit <amount>
/nation bank withdraw <amount>
/nation bank log [page]

Unlike the city tier, nation deposits and withdrawals move funds between the nation treasury and the actor’s personal Vault balance, not between the nation treasury and a city treasury. Player-to-treasury and treasury-to-player are the only flows on either tier; the tax sweep is the one that moves between treasuries.

Tax

Both tiers ship with a tax sweeper that runs on a single executor at tax_scan_interval_minutes (default 15). On each tick the sweeper picks up entities whose own taxIntervalHours has elapsed since their last collection.

City tax

Each member pays taxAmount coins every taxIntervalHours hours (default 24). The founder is exempt when taxFounderExempt is on (default true) — this is a per-city setting separate from the server-wide default_city_tax_founder_exempt.

Configure via the mayor (or someone with EDIT_INFO + BANK): 

/city tax set <amount> <intervalHours>
/city tax founderexempt true|false
/city tax info
/city tax pay              clear your own arrears from personal Vault

intervalHours is clamped to [min_tax_interval_hours, max_tax_interval_hours] (defaults 1–168). Setting amount to 0 disables city tax collection entirely.

On each sweep, for every member except the (optional) exempt founder:

  • If Vault.withdraw succeeds: the treasury is credited, a BankLog row is written with reason tax, and the member’s lastTaxPaidAt updates.
  • If the member is broke: their taxArrears increases by amount, and a chat warning fires when they’re next online. Officer-and-higher ranks get a separate alert.

Use /city tax pay to clear your own arrears from your personal Vault balance in one shot (rejected if you don’t have enough to cover the whole amount). Tax sweeps continue while the city is in GRACE.

Nation tax

The nation sweeper bills cities, not players. Each member city pays taxAmount coins out of its own treasury every taxIntervalHours hours (default 24). The capital is exempt when capitalCityExempt is on (default true). 

/nation tax set <amount> <intervalHours>
/nation tax capitalexempt true|false
/nation tax info
/nation tax pay              clear your city's arrears from city treasury

On each sweep, for every member city except the (optional) exempt capital:

  • If the city’s treasury covers the amount: it’s debited, the nation treasury is credited, and two BankLog rows are written (DEBIT at city tier, CREDIT at nation tier, both reason tax).
  • If the city can’t cover it: memberCity.taxArrears increases by amount, and the city’s mayor is warned in chat.

Any member-city mayor can run /nation tax pay to clear their city’s arrears (drains that city’s treasury; reason tax-pay-arrears).

Bank Log

Every coin movement Nations touches is appended to nations_bank_log. The log is append-only — there is no auto-prune in this version. Each row records:

  • tier — CITY or NATION.
  • cityId / nationId — the affected entity (one is set, the other null).
  • playerUuid — the acting player, or null for system events (tax sweeps, disband refunds).
  • direction — CREDIT or DEBIT.
  • amount — absolute value.
  • balanceAfter — snapshot of the treasury after the move.
  • reason — one of the enumerated reasons below.
  • createdAt — epoch millis.

Admin Overrides

Server admins (holders of questlinesnations.admin) can rewrite treasury balances directly. The override still writes a BankLog row with reason admin for traceability. 

/citiesadmin treasury set <city> <amount>
/nationsadmin treasury set <nation> <amount>

conquest and forced-disband flows write their own bank-log rows automatically — the admin reason is reserved for explicit balance edits.

Founding Costs

Both founding flows charge a non-refundable cost up front, audited as a create-cost bank-log row even though no entity exists yet (the cityId/nationId columns sit null until materialization):

ActionCost keyDefaultPaid from
Found a citycreate_city_cost500Founder’s Vault balance
Found a nationcreate_nation_cost2500Founding city’s treasury

Both costs are non-refundable. Cancelling a pending creation, declining an invitation, or letting the invite TTL expire leaves the cost gone — no automatic rollback. relocate_town_hall_cost and relocate_capital_district_cost exist in config but are currently dormant; see Town Hall & Capital District.
