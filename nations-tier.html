Nations Plugin

Nations

The federation layer above cities. A nation is two or more cities choosing to share a capital, a treasury, and a slice of the map.

The Shape

A nation looks a lot like a city, but with cities as members instead of players. It has its own name, tag, color, and motd; its own claim pool (stored in QL Claims as NATION-typed claims); its own treasury, tax rate, and rank system; and its own diplomacy.

One city — the capital — holds the leadership role. The capital’s mayor speaks for the nation: only they can invite or kick member cities, only they can run a /nation command that requires ADMIN_OVERRIDE. Other member cities elect their own mayors and run their own internal affairs unaffected.

Founding a Nation

Any mayor of any city can found a nation by inviting a second city. No special “found nation” permission — being a mayor is the gate. 

/nation create <cityName> <name> <tag> [#color] [motd...]

The founding city’s treasury pays create_nation_cost (default 2500). Same rule as cities: the cost is non-refundable — if the invitee city’s mayor declines or the invite expires, the coin is gone but no nation exists. Cancel an open invitation with /nation create cancel.

Materialization happens when the invitee city’s mayor runs /nation accept <nationName>. The accept transaction inserts the nation row, seeds its built-in ranks, adds both cities as NationMemberCity rows, and drops the pending row. The founding city becomes the initial capital and is pinned to the Founder nation rank.

ℹ
Both cities must currently be solo

Neither the founder city nor the target city can already be in a nation. A city belongs to at most one nation at a time, just like a player belongs to at most one city.

Member Cities

Each member city is stored as a NationMemberCity row with a nation rank, join time, and tax arrears. All players of a member city inherit that city’s nation rank when acting inside the nation’s chunks. There is no per-player nation rank: the unit of governance at this tier is the city, not the individual.

Joining

The capital mayor invites with: 

/nation invite <cityName>

Both the capital-mayor gate and the absence of any INVITE_CITY permission are deliberate — this is one of the few capabilities not reachable through the nation rank system. The invited city’s mayor runs /nation accept <nationName> to join.

Leaving

The mayor of any non-capital member city runs /nation leave to withdraw their city. The capital city itself can’t just leave — it must first transfer the capital role (see below).

Kicking

/nation kick <cityName> is capital-mayor only. There is no rank perm for kicking a city.

Nation Ranks

A nation seeds three immutable built-in ranks on creation, mirroring the city tier:

Rank Role Mutable?
Capital Founder rank. Held by whatever city is currently the capital. Carries ADMIN_OVERRIDE. Name + flag locked.
Citizen Default rank assigned to newly joined member cities. Name + flag locked.
Allies Governs what allied outsider nations may do in this nation’s chunks. Name + flag locked.

Add custom ranks via /nation rank create <name> <weight> and reassign member cities with /nation setrank <cityName> <rankName>. Like the city tier, the three built-in ranks can have their permissions rewritten but can’t be renamed or deleted.

Nation Permissions

Permissions are split into management, territory, and in-claim groups, mirroring the city tier. There is no INVITE_CITY or KICK_CITY permission — those are hard-gated to the capital mayor at the command layer, not controlled by the rank bitmask.

Permission Group What it grants
MANAGE_RANKSManagementCreate / delete / edit nation ranks; reassign member cities.
EDIT_INFOManagementChange name, tag, motd, color; combined with BANK sets nation tax.
DIPLOMACYManagementPropose / accept / break nation alliances; declare / surrender nation wars.
BANKManagementDeposit and withdraw from the nation treasury.
NATION_CLAIMTerritoryClaim chunks for the nation via /claim map.
NATION_UNCLAIMTerritoryRelease nation chunks via /claim map.
NATION_BUILDIn-claimPlace blocks inside nation chunks.
NATION_BREAKIn-claimBreak blocks inside nation chunks.
NATION_INTERACTIn-claimRight-click doors, signs, buttons, NPCs in nation chunks.
NATION_USE_CONTAINERSIn-claimOpen chests in nation chunks.
ADMIN_OVERRIDEInternalForced on for the Capital rank. Bypasses every other gate inside nation chunks.

Nation permissions only apply inside nation chunks. They do not cascade down into member cities’ own city chunks — a nation cannot grant outsiders (or even other member cities) access to its members’ city land. That distinction is the core of why the model has two separate tiers instead of one.

Capital Transfer

Like the mayor role, the capital is pinned to one specific member — in this case, the member city. Hand the capital off through:

  1. /nation transfer <cityName> — capital mayor opens an offer to another member city.
  2. /nation transfer accept — the target city’s mayor accepts; capital fields swap.
  3. /nation transfer decline — target’s mayor declines.
  4. /nation transfer cancel — capital mayor rescinds.

Offers expire after transfer_offer_ttl_minutes (default 60). The capital cannot run /nation leave while an offer is pending. Accepting an offer is a pure rank swap inside a single transaction: the capital field on the nation row flips to the new city, the old capital drops to the Default nation rank, and the new capital takes the Founder rank. No cost is charged.

ℹ
Capital District is not wired yet

The relocate_capital_district_cost config key and the relocate-cd bank-log reason exist in the data model but aren’t consumed yet. Capital transfer in v3.0.0 is purely a governance change — there is no “Capital District” chunk anchor yet, and no charge is taken from the nation treasury.

Server admins can override the capital directly without consent via /nationsadmin setcapital.

Grace & Disband

When a nation drops below min_nation_cities (default 2) member cities, it enters GRACE:

  • Existing nation claims stay protected.
  • The capital mayor still receives invite responses; recovery is reaching quorum again.
  • The nation tax sweep still runs.
  • Member cities get a periodic broadcast.

Grace expires after grace_days (default 7), at which point the grace-sweep task auto-disbands the nation. The capital mayor can also run /nation disband for an immediate double-confirmed teardown.

Disband releases every nation claim, frees every member city to no-nation state, marks alliances BROKEN and wars ENDED_BY_DISBAND, and (when disband_refund_to_cities is on) splits the nation treasury evenly among released member cities — rounding remainder goes to the former capital. Server admins can force a teardown via /nationsadmin delete <nationName>.

Settings

Identity fields are editable by any actor whose nation rank carries EDIT_INFO:

  • /nation setname <name>
  • /nation settag <tag>
  • /nation setmotd <text>
  • /nation setcolor <#hex>

Tag and color show up in chat as the outer brace of the [NATIONTAG][CITYTAG] prefix, as the title on the entry banner when crossing into a nation chunk, and as the dominant tint on /claim map.

Nation Overview UI

Bare /nation opens the Nation Overview page for your current nation. It mirrors the city overview’s single-page shape, scoped to the nation tier: identity header, motd, a paginated member-city list (rows show city tag, mayor, member count, nation rank), and footer buttons routing to ranks, bank, the claim map, and settings. Per-city actions (set-rank / kick / inspect) open by clicking a city row; the kick action is gated on you being the capital’s mayor.
