Nations Plugin

Diplomacy & Wars

Alliances and wars at both tiers, the relationship resolver behind the banner and map colors, and the rank-based permission model that decides what an allied outsider may do inside your chunks.

Two Independent Tiers

Diplomacy is layered the same way as ownership: city-to-city relations live on the city tier, nation-to-nation relations live on the nation tier, and the two don’t cascade through each other’s membership.

  • A city ally only grants access to that city’s own chunks.
  • A nation ally only grants access to that nation’s own chunks (the federation projects), not to the member cities’ chunks.
  • Member cities of the same nation are implicitly at peace and counted as “Compatriots” — they can’t declare war on each other.

Every relationship is symmetric and binary: you either have an active alliance or you don’t, an active war or you don’t. There are no per-pair custom permission overrides — access is gated by each side’s own Allies rank perms, not by negotiated treaty terms.

Alliances

City alliances

The acting city must have a member with the DIPLOMACY permission for any of these commands:

/city ally <cityName>          propose
/city ally accept <cityName>   accept a pending proposal
/city ally decline <cityName>  reject a pending proposal
/city ally break <cityName>    end an active alliance

A proposal sits in nations_city_alliance with status PROPOSED_BY_A (or PROPOSED_BY_B) until accepted — at which point it flips to ACTIVE — or until alliance_proposal_ttl_hours (default 48) elapses and the sweep task prunes it.

Several rules block proposals:

  • You can’t propose an alliance to a city you’re already at war with — /city war surrender first.
  • You can’t propose an alliance to a co-nation city — you’re already implicitly at peace as compatriots.
  • max_city_alliances_per_city (default 5) caps total active alliances per city.

Nation alliances

Identical shape at the nation tier; the acting member must hold the nation DIPLOMACY permission. 

/nation ally <nationName>
/nation ally accept <nationName>
/nation ally decline <nationName>
/nation ally break <nationName>

max_nation_alliances_per_nation (default 5) caps the count. When broadcast_alliance_formation is on (default), accepting a proposal at either tier sends a server-wide broadcast.

Wars

City wars

Same DIPLOMACY gate; wars become active immediately on declaration:

/city war declare <cityName>       open war (no proposal step)
/city war surrender <cityName>     end with surrender by either side
/city wars                          list your active wars

Wars sit in nations_city_war with status ACTIVE and one of the closing statuses: ENDED_BY_SURRENDER_A, ENDED_BY_SURRENDER_B, ENDED_BY_TREATY, ENDED_BY_ADMIN, or ENDED_BY_DISBAND.

Declaration rules:

  • You can’t declare on a current ally — /city ally break first.
  • You can’t declare on a city you’re already at war with.
  • You can’t declare on a co-nation city.
  • max_concurrent_city_wars (default 3) caps simultaneous active wars per city.
  • war_declaration_cooldown_minutes (default 30) is a soft per-side throttle.

Nation wars

Identical shape at the nation tier:

/nation war declare <nationName>
/nation war surrender <nationName>
/nation wars

max_concurrent_nation_wars (default 3) caps the count. Admins can end either tier’s wars with /nationsadmin war end or /nationsadmin war setstatus; see Admin Commands.

ℹ
Wars do not change protection

Declaring war does not modify permission resolution one bit. It does not turn on PvP, drop claim protection, or grant access to enemy chunks. The only things wars affect are the relationship label shown on the entry banner, the tint on /claim map, and the chat broadcast when broadcast_war_declarations is on. Conquest happens through admin staging after an out-of-band fight; see Conquest.

Relationship Resolver

When an actor enters a chunk owned by some other party, EntryBannerSystem classifies the relationship for the banner and chat line. For a CITY chunk the classifier picks the first match from this order:

  1. CITY_MEMBER — the owning city is your city.
  2. CITY_ENEMY — you’re in an active CityWar with the owning city.
  3. CITY_ALLY — you have an active CityAlliance with the owning city.
  4. NATION_MEMBER (Compatriot) — your city and the owning city are in the same nation.
  5. NATION_ENEMY — your nation has an active NationWar with the owning city’s nation (cascades to all member cities for the banner label).
  6. NATION_ALLY — your nation has an active NationAlliance with theirs (also cascades).
  7. NEUTRAL — none of the above.

For a NATION chunk the classifier collapses to the four nation-level cases: NATION_MEMBER, NATION_ENEMY, NATION_ALLY, NEUTRAL — city-level alliances and wars are not consulted for nation-owned land.

The label never changes what you can or can’t do — that’s done by the rank-based perm checks described next. It only drives the entry banner subtitle, the banner title’s color tint, and the wilderness-aware chat line.

The Allies Rank

Each city and each nation has a built-in Allies rank that is never assigned to any actual member. Its purpose is to act as the permission template for everyone who counts as an ally of that entity.

When an outsider performs an action inside your city’s chunk and the resolver determines they’re a city ally or a co-nation compatriot, Nations gates the action by your city’s Allies-rank perm (not theirs). The default seeded value is CITY_INTERACT only — allies can right-click doors and signs in your city, but can’t break, build, or open chests until you explicitly grant those: 

/city rank perm Allies CITY_BUILD on
/city rank perm Allies CITY_USE_CONTAINERS on

The same model applies at the nation tier. Inside a NATION-typed chunk, allied-nation actions are gated by the owning nation’s Allies rank. Configure it with /nation rank perm Allies <NATION_*> on|off.

Crucially, neither a city nor a nation can grant access to the other tier’s claims through their Allies rank. The city Allies rank only governs CITY_* perms inside city chunks. The nation Allies rank only governs NATION_* perms inside nation chunks.

Neutral Outsiders

An actor who isn’t a member, a co-nation compatriot, or an ally falls into the neutral bucket. What they can do inside a claim is controlled by the server-wide neutral policy in questlinesnations.conf: 

city_neutral_can_enter=true
city_neutral_can_interact=true
city_neutral_can_use_containers=false
city_neutral_can_build=false
city_neutral_can_break=false
nation_neutral_can_enter=true
nation_neutral_can_interact=true
nation_neutral_can_use_containers=false
nation_neutral_can_build=false
nation_neutral_can_break=false

These are server-wide defaults — every city uses the same city_neutral_* policy, every nation uses the same nation_neutral_* policy. A city or nation can be more generous to neutrals than to allies (rare, but possible) by tightening the Allies rank instead of the neutral policy.

Banner & Chat Indicators

EntryBannerSystem polls online players every 500 ms; on a chunk change it shows a brief title-and-subtitle card via Claims’ own notification manager (so it doesn’t compete with the vanilla title slot or Claims’ own banners). Per-player cooldown between banner fires is entry_banner_cooldown_seconds (default 5).

OwnerTitleSubtitle
Nation chunkNationNameNation chunk, or Nation chunk - <label> when the relationship has a label
City chunkCityNameCity chunk, or City chunk - <label>
Player chunk (member of a city)PlayerNameCitizen of CityName
Player chunk (solo owner)PlayerName(empty)

The relationship labels used in the subtitle are:

  • CITY_MEMBER / SELF → “Member territory”
  • NATION_MEMBER → “Compatriot territory”
  • CITY_ALLY / NATION_ALLY → “Allied territory”
  • CITY_ENEMY / NATION_ENEMY → “Enemy territory”
  • NEUTRAL → no label (the subtitle is just “City chunk” / “Nation chunk” with no suffix)

The title text is tinted by relationship: green for member, cyan for compatriot, blue for allied, red for enemy, white for neutral.

On a city or nation chunk, the banner also prints a chat line: 

[CITYTAG] Your territory (OwnerName)            // CITY_MEMBER / SELF
[CITYTAG] Compatriot territory (OwnerName)      // NATION_MEMBER
[CITYTAG] Allied territory (OwnerName)          // ALLY
[CITYTAG] WARNING: enemy territory (OwnerName)  // ENEMY
[CITYTAG] Neutral territory (OwnerName)         // NEUTRAL

Crossing into wilderness only prints a chat line when show_wilderness_chat_on_exit is on (default off), and the line is the fixed string [Nations] Entering wilderness.

Chat prefix

Separately, ChatTagSystem intercepts every PlayerChatEvent and prepends a stacked bracket prefix depending on the speaker’s membership state: 

PlayerName: ...                            // not in a city
[CITYTAG] PlayerName: ...                  // in a solo city
[NATIONTAG][CITYTAG] PlayerName: ...       // in a city that is in a nation

Tags are plain ASCII; the configured city/nation colors are not applied to the chat prefix (they apply to the entry banner title only).
