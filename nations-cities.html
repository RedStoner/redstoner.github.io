Cities

How a city comes into existence, who’s in it, how its ranks and permissions work, and how its leadership changes hands.

Founding a City

Cities are founded by two players together. The founder runs /city create naming the second player; the second player runs /city accept to make it real. 

/city create <playerName> <name> <tag> [#color] [motd...]

The founder pays create_city_cost (default 500) up front from their VaultUnlocked balance. The cost is non-refundable: if the invitee declines or the invite expires, the city is never created but the coins stay spent. Cancel an open invitation with /city create cancel — same rule, the cost is already gone.

While the invite is open, no city row exists. A PendingCityCreation row sits in the database with the founder, invitee, name, tag, color, and motd. Only when the invitee runs: 

/city accept <cityName>

… does the city actually materialize. That single command opens a transaction that inserts the city row, its four built-in ranks, both members, and deletes the pending row — if any step fails the whole thing rolls back.

ℹ
Name and tag rules

Names are 3–24 characters, alphanumeric plus space. Tags are 2–5 characters, automatically uppercased. Both must be unique across all existing cities and all pending creations — you can’t reserve a name by pending it forever.

Members

A player belongs to at most one city at a time. Membership is stored as a CityMember row keyed by player UUID, carrying their cached username, current rank, join time, and tax arrears.

Joining

Existing cities invite new players via /city invite <player> (requires the INVITE_PLAYER perm); the player accepts with /city accept <cityName>. Joining puts them on the Default rank (the built-in “Citizen”).

The same /city accept command handles both pending-creation acceptances and ordinary invites — it figures out which applies based on whether your UUID is the invitee on a pending creation or has an InvitePending row to an existing city of that name.

Accepting any invite drops every other pending invite you held, so you don’t accidentally keep stale invitations sitting around.

Leaving

/city leave removes you from your city and drops all your Share with City grants. The mayor cannot leave directly — they must first transfer the role (see below). When membership drops below min_city_members (default 2), the city enters GRACE.

Kicking

/city kick <player> requires the KICK_MEMBER perm. You can’t kick someone whose rank weight is at or above yours — the “Allies” rank is pinned at weight 0 and is not a real member, so it never comes up.

Ranks

Every city has a permission system built from per-rank bitmasks. When a city is created four ranks are seeded automatically — three of them immutable (you can edit their permissions but not delete or rename them), one of them just a seed you can change however you like.

Rank Weight Role Default permissions Mutable?
Mayor 100 Founder rank. Always held by exactly one player — the current mayor. All permissions, including ADMIN_OVERRIDE. Name + flag locked; ADMIN_OVERRIDE cannot be toggled off.
Officer 50 Seeded mid-tier rank for trusted members. INVITE_PLAYER, KICK_MEMBER, EDIT_INFO, BANK, CITY_CLAIM, CITY_UNCLAIM, CITY_BUILD, CITY_BREAK, CITY_INTERACT, CITY_USE_CONTAINERS, MANAGE_PLOTS. Fully editable — rename, delete, or rewrite perms.
Citizen 10 Default rank assigned to every new joiner. None. Name + flag locked; permissions editable.
Allies 0 Governs what allied outsiders may do in this city’s chunks. Not held by any actual player. CITY_INTERACT only. Name + flag locked; permissions editable.

Add custom ranks with /city rank create <name> <weight>, edit any rank’s bitmask with /city rank perm <rank> <perm> on|off, and move members between ranks with /city setrank <player> <rank>. Custom ranks can be renamed or deleted; the three immutable ones cannot.

Mayor Transfer

The mayor is pinned to whichever member holds the Mayor rank. There is always exactly one. The role is handed off through an offer-and-accept flow so no one can be made mayor against their will:

  1. /city transfer <player> — mayor opens an offer to a member.
  2. /city transfer accept — target accepts; in one transaction the previous mayor moves to the Default rank, the new mayor takes the Founder rank, and the pending fields clear.
  3. /city transfer decline — target rejects.
  4. /city transfer cancel — incumbent rescinds an open offer.

Offers expire after transfer_offer_ttl_minutes (default 60). The mayor cannot run /city leave while an offer is pending — resolve or cancel the transfer first. Server admins can override the mayor directly without consent via /citiesadmin setmayor.

Grace & Disband

When a city drops below min_city_members (default 2) it enters the GRACE state. While in grace:

  • Existing claims are still protected.
  • New claims and invites still flow normally — the city can recover by reaching quorum again.
  • The tax sweeper continues running.
  • Remaining members get a periodic broadcast warning the city is about to disband.

If the city stays under quorum past grace_days (default 7) the grace-sweep task auto-disbands it. /city disband run by the mayor — double-confirmed — does the same thing immediately. Either path releases every city claim, ejects every member, drops every share grant for the city, marks alliances BROKEN, marks wars ENDED_BY_DISBAND, and (when disband_refund_to_members is on) splits the treasury evenly among remaining members — with the rounding remainder going to the mayor.

Server admins can force-disband a stuck city without grace via /citiesadmin delete <cityName>.

Settings

The basic identity of a city is editable any time by holders of EDIT_INFO:

  • /city setname <name> — same uniqueness rules as creation.
  • /city settag <tag> — uppercased; same scope.
  • /city setmotd <text> — 200 char cap.
  • /city setcolor <#hex> — affects map tint, banner, and chat tag.

Color changes propagate instantly to the entry banner, the chat-tag prefix, and /claim map’s tinting.

City Overview UI

Typing bare /city opens the City Overview page (CityOverviewUI) for your current city. It’s a single-page layout with the city name and tag in the header, the motd, a paginated member list (8 per page, sorted by rank with the mayor first, then by join date), and a row of action buttons in the footer that route to the ranks panel, the bank, the claim map (via /claim map), and the settings panel.

Most everyday operations — setting ranks, kicking, transferring mayor — are reached by clicking a member row, which opens a per-member action panel. The CLI subcommands listed under Commands remain available for scripting, automation, and quick one-offs.
